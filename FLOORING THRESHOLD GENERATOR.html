01 — PROFILE GEOMETRY

Select a vertex and click Lock to pin its X/Y position. Locked points cannot move during dragging, line edits, or coordinate changes. When an I/H or D/E member is pinned, both members hold their Y coordinates. Unlock to edit. Click or drag a vertex to edit its X/Y coordinates or add a fillet. Drag a line to move both endpoints; click its two-letter label to edit its length. New J–N vertices follow the left-to-right top contour. Changing their count redistributes them; the curve passes through each control point.

Individual line lengths

Length is the straight-line distance between the named vertices. When one endpoint is locked, the other endpoint adjusts; when both endpoints are locked, the segment is fixed.
Print-flat T-cap: low, flat textured face for printing face-down on the build plate; an upper strip tapers into a central anchoring stem. Flat faces print easiest with texture disabled or extremely shallow. Test-fit and verify fastening strength before use.

02 — VERTEX COORDINATES

Choose any labeled sketch node (A–I, plus optional J–N) or enter its X and Y here. X is horizontal across the threshold; Y is vertical above the floor datum. Every corner has its own editable coordinates; the selected curve setting controls the upper profile between top vertices. I ↔ H and D ↔ E share Y only. B (crest) is independent; all X coordinates remain independently editable.
